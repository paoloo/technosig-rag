# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes thoughtful participation from people of every background and level of experience.

Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep technical discussion focused on improving the work. Disagreement is welcome; harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, and the publication of another person's private information are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Decisions should be proportionate, documented where appropriate, and centered on keeping the project safe and constructive.

To report unacceptable behavior privately, email `paolocmo@gmail.com`. Reports will be handled with discretion, although complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when investigation or safety requires involving others.
